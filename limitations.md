# Methodological Limitations and Empirical Pilot Scope

This document provides a comprehensive accounting of the methodological boundaries, probe suite characteristics, and empirical scope of this study on DistilBERT compression, following senior NLP-efficiency researcher and IEEE conference reviewer standards.

## 1. Scope of the Empirical Study
- **Pilot Evaluation Rather Than General Benchmark:** The contextual comprehension evaluation uses a curated 16-prompt masked-token qualitative probe suite spanning four semantic categories (syntax/agreement, common-sense world knowledge, technological reasoning, and complex relations), and contextual embedding stability is evaluated on 5 sentence pairs (10 sentences). These probes characterize representation fidelity in a controlled setting, but do not substitute for comprehensive downstream evaluation on benchmarks such as GLUE (MNLI, SST-2, QQP) or SQuAD.
- **Single Architecture Scope:** All experiments evaluate `distilbert-base-uncased` (6 layers, 12 heads, hidden dimension 768, 66,985,530 total parameters, 66,362,880 encoder parameters). Findings cannot be generalized to larger models (e.g., BERT-Large, RoBERTa), decoder-only architectures (e.g., LLaMA, Mistral, GPT-2), or multi-modal models without separate empirical validation.
- **One-Shot Post-Training Pruning:** Pruning is performed strictly one-shot on pretrained weights without iterative pruning-retraining cycles or sparse pretraining. This work does not extend or reproduce *Prune Once for All* (Zafrir et al., 2021), which relies on extensive sparse pretraining.

## 2. Quantization and Hardware Realities
- **Simulated (Fake) INT4 Quantization:** The 4-bit uniform per-output-channel quantization is simulated via fake quantization (weight clamping to $[-8, 7]$ and immediate dequantization back to floating-point). It does not employ packed 4-bit integer storage formats, real INT4 matrix-multiplication kernels (such as CUTLASS or bitsandbytes), or hardware acceleration.
- **Theoretical Footprint:** The reported 31.94 MiB footprint for INT4 is a computed mathematical payload ($66,985,530 \times 4 / 8 / 1024^2$), labeled as theoretical, and excludes scale factor overhead, module metadata, and serialization container headers.
- **Dense Execution of Sparse Weights:** All sparse models are evaluated within standard dense tensor containers. As demonstrated by our controlled CPU latency benchmark (170.33 ms dense FP32 vs. 170.87 ms at 45% Global L1 sparsity), unstructured sparsity yields zero acceleration on standard dense BLAS/DNN kernels. No latency speedup from unstructured sparsity is claimed.
- **Dynamic INT8 Footprint Invariance:** Serialization of PyTorch `quantize_dynamic` models produces a 154.76 MB container regardless of sparsity level because pruned zero weights are stored as explicit quantized zeros in dense matrices.

## 3. Implementation Details and Ablations Disclosed
- **Distillation Scope:** Distillation recovery implements logit Kullback-Leibler divergence ($T=2.0$, AdamW $lr=3\times 10^{-5}$, 3 epochs, 8 domain sentences, exact gradient zero-masking). Intermediate hidden-state matching and attention-map matching are not implemented in the codebase and are explicitly not claimed.
- **Structured Pruning Implementation Artifact:** The original notebook's `prune_structured_ln` applied $L_2$-norm row pruning to all `nn.Linear` modules, inadvertently pruning rows of `vocab_projector` ($30522 \times 768$), which deletes output vocabulary logits. In our Step 0 execution, we evaluated both the encoder-only structured pruning (main result) and the all-Linear implementation (reported as an ablation), finding that both collapse to 0.00% accuracy due to channel removal without fine-tuning.
- **Representation Metric Scope:** Embedding stability is measured via mean cosine similarity of mean-pooled embeddings across sentence pairs. While cosine similarity provides a fast metric of representation drift, it does not detect dimensional collapse or anisotropy; complementary metrics such as Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) should be incorporated in future full-scale studies.
